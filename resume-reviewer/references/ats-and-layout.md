# ATS and Layout Guide

## Application Structure

- Use reverse chronology for online submissions.
- Use standard labels such as `Education`, `Experience`, `Projects`, and `Skills`.
- Keep name, contact information, titles, employers, locations, dates, education, and keywords in normal body text.
- Avoid tables, text boxes, sidebars, icons, graphics, and headers or footers for essential information.
- Keep date formats consistent, preferably `Mon YYYY - Mon YYYY` or `Mon YYYY - Present`.
- Spell out uncommon acronym on first use, followed by abbreviation.
- Keep `Skills` as final section under this project's explicit hard rule.

## Section Order

Choose strongest evidence order while keeping Skills last.

### Typical New Graduate

1. Contact information
2. Optional target title or concise evidence-backed summary
3. Education
4. Experience
5. Projects
6. Research, awards, publications, or relevant leadership
7. Skills

### New Graduate With Strong Internship Experience

1. Contact information
2. Optional target title or concise evidence-backed summary
3. Experience
4. Education
5. Projects or research
6. Skills

Omit generic summary when it repeats education or skills without proof.

## Content and Length

- Use one page when light experience fits clearly; use two pages when relevant evidence justifies space.
- Prioritize recent and target-relevant proof. Do not force every role into identical bullet count.
- Keep unrelated work when it proves target requirements or maintains useful chronology; otherwise reduce or omit it.
- Convert coursework into project or applied evidence instead of listing many course titles.
- Omit high school after college begins unless exceptional, directly relevant evidence requires it.

## Keywords

- Mirror exact posting terminology only when it truthfully describes candidate evidence.
- Prove important keywords in experience or project bullets; do not rely on keyword list alone.
- Avoid hidden text, repeated keyword blocks, and unnatural noun lists.
- Preserve official titles. Add clarifying functional title only when transparent, for example `Software Engineering Intern (official title: Technology Intern)`.

## Contact, Privacy, and Exclusions

- Include name, city/state, professional email, phone, LinkedIn, and GitHub or portfolio when useful.
- Exclude photo, birth date, full street address, Social Security number, marital status, salary history, and confidential employer data.
- Exclude `References available upon request`; keep references on separate document if requested.

## Formatting

- Use readable 10-12 point body type, visible hierarchy, consistent spacing, and restrained emphasis in final DOCX or PDF.
- Use simple bullet symbols and left alignment.
- Follow employer file-format instructions. When unspecified, provide clean DOCX or PDF and verify parsed fields after upload.
- Treat Markdown as content draft, not proof of final line length or ATS rendering.
- Render final DOCX/PDF and inspect wrapping, clipped text, spacing, page breaks, and widows/orphans.
